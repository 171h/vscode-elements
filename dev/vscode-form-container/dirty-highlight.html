Modified state of the form container

Modify a control of a form, the background of every form control of that form turns light blue. Only one form is highlighted at a time: modify another form and the previous one returns to its normal state immediately.

The light theme uses #eff3ff. The dark and the high contrast themes use the matching tones of the palette, which the playground theme selector below applies to the page.
