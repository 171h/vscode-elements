Percentage mode

Opacity

Typed text: 50%   Value read by the program: 0.5

Editing

The percent sign is a mask: it appears as soon as the number has a digit, 05 becomes 5% when the field loses the focus, and a typed comma is converted to a period.

Zoom (required)

Validation

min, max, and step use the fraction form, so min="0" max="1" accepts 0% - 100% and step="0.05" means steps of 5%.

Valid: true
